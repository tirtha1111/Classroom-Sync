Since browser screenshots require live user sessions, open the live app link above, log in with the Teacher Demo or Student Demo buttons, and take screenshots of the 6 key screens outlined below for your presentation slides!

SLIDE 01 / (Landing)

Landing & Role Selection

Professional EdTech landing page with value proposition and instant role onboarding for students and teachers.

Action: Click "Continue as Teacher" or "Continue as Student".
SLIDE 02 /teacher

Teacher Dashboard

Class setup form (Class XII-A, Physics, Topic) and recent session history management.

Action: Enter topic & click "START CLASS".
SLIDE 03 /classroom/:id

Live Teacher Whiteboard

Full canvas drawing workspace with pen, highlighter, eraser, text, color selector, stroke widths, and End Class button.

Action: Draw equations or notes live on screen.
SLIDE 04 /student

Student Dashboard

"Live Now" pulsing indicator card for active classes and searchable archive of past sessions.

Action: Click "JOIN CLASS" to enter live session.
SLIDE 05 /classroom/:id (Student)

Student Live View

Real-time sync view where student watches teacher's strokes appear instantly with view-only permissions.

Action: Watch strokes sync in real-time.
SLIDE 06 /session/:id

Archived Class Session

Post-class view displaying session metadata and exact preserved whiteboard work for catch-up study.

Action: Open any completed class from recent sessions.
